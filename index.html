About Us

Our Guiding Compass and Vision

At Evolving Minds, the mission is to empower individuals by fostering a secure and inclusive environment for self-exploration, understanding and personal growth.

The clinic prioritises each person's transformative journey, using validated, evidence-based psychological therapies designed around individual requirements.

Our Goal

Collaborate with each person to support meaningful progress and practical change.

Meet The Team

Guiding you every step of the way

Shagun Gulgulia (Founder)

Clinical Psychologist - M.Psych (Clinical), MAPS and Board Approved Clinical Supervisor

"Connection is at the heart of healing. Helping individuals create positive change in their lives and witnessing the ripple effect it has on their loved ones is what truly motivates me."

Shagun leverages evidence-based practices to support meaningful outcomes with children and adolescents. Her approach is neurodivergent-affirming, strengths-based and focused on helping young people and families build practical skills for home, school and social environments.

Jacinta Yamine

Clinical Psychology Registrar

Jacinta supports children, adolescents and families, with a focus on early intervention for anxiety, mood and behavioural challenges. She also brings adult mental health experience across inpatient and outpatient settings.

Danielle Brooke

Registered Psychologist

Danielle creates a safe, collaborative space for people across the lifespan. She has specialised in supporting children and families affected by trauma, using CBT, ACT and trauma-informed, attachment-based practices.

Mika Colbert

Provisional Psychologist

Mika supports children, adolescents and families, with particular interest in anxiety and social difficulties. She helps facilitate group therapy in a compassionate, welcoming environment.

Jo Smith

Reception and Admin Coordinator

Jo is often the first point of contact at reception, coordinating appointments, client intake and the daily operations that help families feel supported from the beginning.

Client Information

Before your first appointment

Arrival

Please arrive 10 minutes before your scheduled appointment to complete any necessary paperwork. Each appointment lasts 50 minutes.

Availability

After-hours and Saturday appointments are available. Bring previous assessments or relevant reports if you have them.

Fees

Fees are based on the structure recommended by the Australian Psychological Society. Full payment is required at consultation.

Medicare Rebates

Eligible referrals may receive rebates for up to 10 individual psychology sessions per calendar year.

Cancellation Policy

The clinic has a 48-hour cancellation and rescheduling policy. Late cancellations are subject to the full fee.

Separated Parent Policy

The clinic prioritises the emotional and psychological wellbeing of children while supporting separated families.
